Jeremy Segal is a musician from Perth, Western Australia

He works primarily with his computer

He is interested in pop, plunderphonics, and media circulation



Jeremy collaborates with domingo castillo flores under the moniker SPDM

The pair perform and broadcast live remixes of their recent media consumption

They have presented work at/on 90mil, Cashmere Radio, Diffrakt Centre for Theoretical Periphery, Jolt Radio, Psychic Liberation, and Radiophrenia



Jeremy cut his teeth playing in/producing/writing songs for indie-rock bands including Segue and Brix

He sometimes works as a recording and/or mixing engineer



Jeremy studied Sound Studies and Sonic Arts at Berlin University of the Arts

He is currently pursuing a PhD at the University of Western Australia



Jeremy would love to hear from you about collaborating, playing a show, producing/mixing your record, teaching a class, running a workshop, music you’re listening to, etc…
